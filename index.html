    Alt spec: Discipline

    Dual spec: unlocks at 40; change talents at a class trainer in a major city. Blizzard deep dive recap · LFCarry. Cost: unconfirmed for Forever.

    Level 30 healer build (21/0/0, tested in beta): Wand Specialization 2, Twin Disciplines 5, Mental Strength 4, Mental Agility 1, Inner Focus 1, Improved Power Word: Shield 3, Meditation 3, Soul Warding 1, Penance 1.

    Level 60 projected (31/20/0): adds Renewed Hope 5, Divine Aegis 3, Power Infusion 1 (40), with Binding Heal from Holy.

    Key Forever-new Disc talents: Penance (21-pt, level 30: heal or damage channel, 12 s CD), Soul Warding (16-pt: removes Power Word: Shield's 4 s CD, -15% cost), Renewed Hope, Divine Aegis (crit-heal shield, caps 1200), Power in Light (Smite/Penance bonus vs your SW:P or Holy Fire).

    Dungeon healing tips: Shield the tank before the pull; Renew + Penance as your main heals; Inner Focus for a free big heal; Meditation keeps mana flowing. Shadowform blocks heals, so swap to Disc spec for healer runs.

    WoW Forever DB Discipline talents (build 1.60.1.70658).

    Stats, dungeons, addons

    Stat priority (Shadow leveling): Shadow spell power → Hit/crit → Intellect, Stamina, Spirit. Wand DPS matters on its own (wands don't scale with spell damage). WoW Forever DB

    Shadow in dungeons: SW:P on everything, Mind Blast on CD but watch threat; Vampiric Embrace heals the party (cooldown cut to 30 s in a beta build, Hyjal). Dark Inspiration (new, replaced Improved Fade) buffs party Spirit.

    Alliance dungeons: Hall of Thanes 13-18 (new), Deadmines, Wailing Caverns, SFK, BFD, Stockade, Gnomeregan, Excavation Site: Wetlands 26-31 (new), SM, Drowned City 35-40 (new), Krol'dok 40-45 (new), Alcaz Prison 48-53 (new), Blackmaw Hold 55-60 (new), Shaper's Terrace 58-60 (new). WoW Forever DB

    Addons tagged for 1.60.1 on CurseForge: Questie, Ding! leveling guide, Dungeon Questie (beta), Play It Forward.

    Forever priest changes: Smite does about half its Classic damage; Shadowform also gives 15% less physical damage taken and +100% Shadow crit bonus; Spirit Tap is now a 100% proc at 5/5; Wand Spec is 2 ranks for 25%. WoW Forever DB talents
